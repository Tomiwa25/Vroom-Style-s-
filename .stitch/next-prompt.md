A high-fashion, editorial runway lookbook and interactive silhouette showroom for "Vroom Styles - Autumn/Winter Atelier Capsule".

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- Brand: Vroom Styles — Haute Warmth Fashion Atelier
- Theme: Light, Warm Editorial Luxury
- Colors:
  * Canvas: #FAF8F5 (Warm Ecru Canvas)
  * Surface Pure: #FFFFFF (Pure Silk)
  * Surface Secondary: #F3EFEA (Oat Wash)
  * Ink Primary: #111111 (Deep Obsidian)
  * Ink Secondary: #4D4843 (Espresso Slate)
  * Accent: #A84B2C (Warm Terracotta Atelier)
  * Hairline Borders: #EBE6DF (Textile Hairline)
- Typography:
  * Display & Headlines: Bodoni Moda (didone serifs, high fashion pacing)
  * UI & Body: Plus Jakarta Sans (clean, rhythmic modern sans-serif)
  * Labels & Micro-UI: Uppercase, 0.14em tracking
- Shapes & Geometry: Architectural sharp edges (0px - 4px radius), 3:4 portrait ratios
- Anti-Clutter Rules: Strictly luxury apparel, tailoring, silks, cashmere, and leathercraft. NO non-fashion goods.

**Page Structure:**
1. Atelier Announcement & Global Navigation:
   - Announcement banner with capsule badge & complimentary shipping notice
   - Minimalist header with "Vroom Styles Lagos • Since 2021" wordmark, lookbook switcher ("Autumn/Winter Capsule", "Resort Archive"), currency toggle, bag drawer trigger
2. Lookbook Hero Cover:
   - Full-bleed or large editorial split-screen with editorial runway title: "Silhouette & Texture: The Autumn/Winter Atelier Study"
   - Subtitle: "Twelve deliberate garments documented between Lagos and Milan. Photographed in natural dusk light."
3. Interactive Runway Editorial Gallery:
   - Series of 6 asymmetric editorial spreads combining full-body runway looks with close-up textile macros (mulberry silk weave, heavy Italian wool grain, calfskin stitch)
