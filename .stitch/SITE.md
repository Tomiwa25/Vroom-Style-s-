# Vroom Styles — Site Constitution & Roadmap (SITE.md)

## 1. Vision & Purpose
Vroom Styles is a premier, contemporary fashion and clothing e-commerce platform offering tailored apparel, fluid silk silhouettes, sculptural knitwear, outerwear, and curated artisanal accessories.

The platform embodies modern fashion commerce: editorial imagery, serene typography, seamless cart drawer, instant quick-view, multi-currency support (NGN/USD), and fluid checkout.
